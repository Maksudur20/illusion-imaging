About Illusion Imaging

A Modern Enterprise Built on Technical Rigor & Integrity

Illusion Imaging is a dynamic commercial enterprise operating across two vital disciplines: Solar Energy System Sales & Services and Medical Equipment Import, Supply & Service.

Operating out of Dakshinkhan, Dhaka, our mission is to empower residential, commercial, industrial, and clinical institutions with high-efficiency energy systems and precision biomedical equipment. We believe that clean electricity and accurate diagnostic technology are the fundamental building blocks of a healthier, more sustainable Bangladesh.

We take pride in our end-to-end service philosophy: whether we are engineering a 100kW on-grid solar array or commissioning a multi-channel ICU patient monitoring system, our certified engineers and technicians oversee every phase from initial site assessment to after-sales support.

Solar Division Services

Complete Solar Energy Engineering Solutions

From residential rooftop systems to large-scale commercial power generation, we supply Tier-1 solar components, perform precision structural installations, and provide proactive maintenance.

Solar Energy Systems Engineering

Complete customized solar engineering for residential, agricultural, and industrial properties. We engineer on-grid net metering setups, resilient off-grid battery systems, and intelligent hybrid systems.

  • On-grid net metering systems
  • Off-grid battery storage setups
  • Smart hybrid energy management

Solar Product Sales

Direct sales of premium, certified solar hardware. We distribute high-efficiency monocrystalline solar panels, pure sine wave hybrid inverters, and long-life LiFePO4 lithium batteries.

  • Tier-1 Monocrystalline modules (550W+)
  • Hybrid inverters (5kW to 15kW)
  • Deep-cycle LiFePO4 batteries

Professional Installation

Certified electrical and structural installation by seasoned solar technicians. Includes roof load analysis, anodized aluminum framing, heavy-duty DC cabling, grounding, and grid synchronization.

  • Anodized corrosion-resistant mounting
  • Surge and lightning arrestor protection
  • Turnkey commissioning & testing

Maintenance & Performance Auditing

Routine preventative maintenance agreements, thermal imaging hotspot detection, inverter firmware upgrades, and panel cleaning services to ensure peak energy yield throughout the system lifetime.

  • Thermal hotspot detection
  • Inverter diagnostics & recalibration
  • Periodic output optimization

Solar Water Pumping & Irrigation

Engineered solar pumping systems replacing noisy diesel engines for agricultural irrigation, poultry farms, fish hatcheries, and community clean drinking water projects.

  • Submersible & surface DC pumps
  • Automatic MPPT pump controllers
  • Dry-run protection sensors

Commercial Solar Street Lighting

All-in-one commercial solar streetlights equipped with long-range radar sensors, high-lumen LED modules, and integrated lithium batteries for campuses, housing societies, and industrial perimeters.

  • IP65 all-weather aluminum housing
  • Smart dusk-to-dawn sensors
  • Zero grid cabling overhead
Medical Division Services

Medical Equipment Import, Supply & Biomedical Services

Supplying clinics, diagnostic centers, and hospital departments with advanced medical technology, authentic spare parts, and dependable biomedical engineering maintenance.

Medical Equipment Import

Direct international importation of certified diagnostic and hospital equipment from leading manufacturers. We handle complete international logistics, quality clearance, and customs handling.

  • Direct procurement from verified factories
  • Regulatory standards compliance
  • Safe climate-controlled transit

Clinical Equipment Supply

Distributing vital diagnostic devices including digital ultrasound scanners, multi-parameter ICU patient monitors, mobile X-ray units, 12-lead ECG machines, and hospital autoclaves.

  • Diagnostic imaging instrumentation
  • Critical care patient monitors
  • Sterilization & laboratory units

Biomedical Equipment Service

Expert diagnostic troubleshooting, circuit repairs, sensor replacements, and calibration for medical equipment. Our trained technicians ensure medical precision and clinical safety.

  • Electronic PCB & sensor repairs
  • Signal & probe troubleshooting
  • Emergency on-call technical response

Clinical Installation & Staff Training

Meticulous placement, proper hospital-grade grounding, power conditioning, and operational training for doctors, nurses, and radiographers to ensure confident operation.

  • Hospital site preparation & isolation
  • Operational user training for staff
  • Handover documentation & protocol setup

Calibration & Routine Maintenance

Scheduled preventative maintenance (PPM), output energy verification, leak detection, and electrical safety testing adhering to healthcare facility compliance requirements.

  • Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)
  • Official calibration verification
  • Reduced downtime guarantee

Authentic Spare Parts & Accessories

Fast sourcing and inventory of original transducers, ECG cables, SpO2 reusable/disposable sensors, NIBP cuffs, ultrasound gels, and thermal recording paper.

  • OEM ultrasound probes & adapters

  • Rapid dispatch from Dhaka inventory
