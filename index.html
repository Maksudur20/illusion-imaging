About Illusion Imaging

A Unified Commitment to Energy Innovation & Healthcare Precision

Illusion Imaging was established to address two vital pillars of modern development: reliable clean renewable energy and state-of-the-art diagnostic medical technology. Headquartered in Dakshinkhan, Dhaka, we provide comprehensive sales, direct import, professional installation, and lifelong technical maintenance.

Division 01

Solar Energy System Sales & Services

High-efficiency solar solutions engineered to reduce electricity bills, eliminate blackout interruptions, and minimize carbon footprints.

Turnkey Solar Systems: On-grid net metering, off-grid storage, and intelligent hybrid systems.
Hardware Sales: Monocrystalline panels, pure sine wave inverters, and LiFePO4 batteries.
Engineering & Installation: Rooftop load calculations, precision mounting, wiring & commissioning.
Maintenance & Audits: Preventative checkups, inverter troubleshooting, and performance tuning.
